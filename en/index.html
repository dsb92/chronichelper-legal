ChronicHelper – support

Help, contact details and answers to the most common questions about the app.

ChronicHelper is a Danish app and its content is in Danish. This English version is provided for convenience; in case of discrepancy the Danish version prevails.

What is ChronicHelper?

ChronicHelper is a free Danish app for students and employees living with a chronic illness. The app brings together:

  • Articles about rights, support options and everyday life with a chronic illness, with links to Danish public-sector websites.
  • Mindfulness exercises as videos (played via YouTube), which you can mark as completed.
  • An anonymous forum where you can share experiences and ask questions to others in the same situation.
  • Notifications about new posts, replies and exercises – only if you turn them on.

The app is free and funded by ads from Google AdMob. It is published by DAB-IT for iOS and Android.

Important: The content in ChronicHelper is general information and shared experiences. It is not medical or legal advice. Always contact your doctor, your educational institution, your employer or the relevant authority if you need specific advice. In an emergency in Denmark, call 112.

Frequently asked questions

Why don't I have a username and password?

ChronicHelper uses anonymous accounts. When you complete the onboarding, an anonymous account bound to your phone is created automatically. You only choose a nickname, an avatar, a user group and optionally an education. We never ask for e-mail, phone number, national ID number or real name.

I got a new phone, deleted the app or reset it – where is my profile?

Because the account is anonymous and bound to the app's local session on your device, it cannot be recovered if the session is lost. This happens if you delete the app, change phone, reset the phone or clear the app's data. You can create a new profile in seconds, but your old posts, comments and completed exercises cannot be transferred. The old profile remains in the system until it is deleted – write to us (see the question about deletion) and we will assess whether we can delete it.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app and go to Mere (More) → Profil → Slet profil. Confirm, and the profile is disabled immediately; your posts, comments, likes, blocks, exercise completions and notification registrations are removed, and the account itself is deleted by an automatic process within minutes. If you no longer have access to the app, e-mail developer@dab-it.dk with your nickname and details that let us recognise the profile. Read more on the Delete account page.

How can I identify my profile to you?

The app does not show a user ID. Write your nickname, your user group, roughly when you created the profile and the wording and time of a post that only the owner would know. If you turned notifications on and noted your installation ID (a random code the app assigns to your installation) you can include that too. We only delete or disclose if the profile can be identified with reasonable certainty. Never share such codes publicly.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

In the app: Mere → Notifikationer. You can also turn off notifications for the whole app in your phone's settings (iOS: Settings → Notifications → ChronicHelper; Android: Settings → Apps → ChronicHelper → Notifications). When notifications are off, we do not send messages to your device and we no longer store your push token.

I don't receive notifications even though they are on

Check that ChronicHelper is allowed to send notifications in your phone's settings, that Do Not Disturb/Focus mode is not active, and that the phone has an internet connection. On Android, battery optimisation may delay messages. Turn notifications off and on again in the app to re-register the device. If that does not help, write to us.

Why are there ads, and how do I change my ad/consent settings?

The app is free and funded by ads via Google AdMob. The first time you use the app (and whenever the rules require it), you are asked for consent to personalised ads through Google's consent dialog (UMP). If you decline, you get non-personalised ads. On iOS you are additionally asked via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

You can change your choice at any time in the app under Mere → Privatlivsindstillinger (shown when required in your region). On iOS you can also go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android to Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads), where you can delete or reset your advertising ID. Read more in the privacy policy.

How do I report or block a user or a post?

Open the post or comment and choose Anmeld (Report) in the menu. Choose a reason and confirm. Posts and comments reported by three different users can be hidden automatically (channels and profiles are always reviewed by a moderator), and we review reports within 24 hours on weekdays. You can also block a user so you no longer see their content and do not receive notifications from them. See the forum rules.

Who can see what I write in the forum?

All users of the app can see posts and comments in the forum. You appear only with your nickname and avatar. Never write information that could identify you or others (name, address, national ID number, workplace, school, etc.), and keep in mind that health information you write yourself becomes visible to others.

Which versions are supported?

ChronicHelper supports iPhone and Android phones with the system versions listed on the App Store and Google Play. Older versions of the app may be asked to update before they can be used.
