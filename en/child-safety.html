Child safety standards

Last updated 30 October 2026 · The Danish version prevails

ChronicHelper has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE, including child sexual abuse material, CSAM). This page describes our standards, how to report, and what we do.

1. What is prohibited

  • Sexual content that involves or sexualises anyone under 18, in text, images, links or any other form.
  • Grooming, attempts to obtain sexual contact with children, or soliciting intimate images.
  • Sharing links to, or requesting, CSAM or any other illegal exploitation of children.
  • Information that could identify or locate a child for the purpose of abuse.

2. How the app is designed

  • The forum is public and text-only. There are no private messages and no uploads of images, video or files.
  • Users are anonymous with a self-chosen nickname. The minimum age is stated in the terms.
  • All forum content can be reported, and users can block others.

3. How to report

  • In the app: open the menu (⋯) on a post, comment, channel or profile and choose Anmeld (report).
  • By e-mail: developer@dab-it.dk. Include the text and time of the post. Never send the material itself to us; contact the police instead.

4. What we do

  • Posts and comments reported by several users can be hidden automatically. All reports, including of channels and profiles, are reviewed by a person; child-safety reports are handled with the highest priority and no later than the next business day.
  • We remove the content, suspend or delete the profile and try to prevent circumvention with a new profile. Because profiles are anonymous we cannot guarantee that this succeeds.
  • We preserve relevant information – when a profile whose content was reported or hidden is deleted, we keep a copy of the content, the nickname and the reports for 90 days (longer if an authority asks) – and report confirmed material and cases to the relevant authorities, including the police, and cooperate with authorities as required by law.

5. Contact

Contact person for questions about child safety and our procedures: DAB-IT, developer@dab-it.dk.
